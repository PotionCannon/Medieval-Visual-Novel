SPIRITUAL HEALING — AUTHOR'S RULES

Healing is not telekinetic control of blood, fluids, or flesh. A healer's spiritual power ORGANIZES AND GUIDES the body's cells and existing natural repair processes, directing what those cells would ordinarily do. Skill determines the complexity of healing the healer can guide. This is a behind-the-scenes consistency rule, NOT a technical speech to put into the script.

Healing also follows TRUE DESIRE, not merely declared intention. A healer who genuinely wants the patient's good can restore tissue almost seamlessly, perhaps leaving only a difference in color rather than raised scar tissue. Self-oriented, impure desires distort the repair. A human healer treating a Draconian might inadvertently create human-like tissue in a wound if motivated selfishly. A healer who secretly wants a hated patient to suffer may inflict painful or distorted healing even while outwardly intending to help. This is not a separate power to reshape bodies at will; it is healing distorted by motive.

Integrity, intention, self-examination, and genuine care are fundamental professional training. A healer can recognize that their desires are unsafe and refuse or hand over a patient rather than risk harming them. There are typically TWO unrelated healers serving together, reducing the chance that both are personally compromised regarding the same patient. The main healer is training her unrelated male apprentice as the second healer. He can handle cuts, bruises, and extremely minor deeper cuts, not knife wounds to the abdomen or severe internal damage.

This worldview is shown through character actions and lessons, not heavy exposition. The lesson to the apprentice should teach that a patient's life must not depend on whether one particular healer can set aside their feelings quickly enough.
