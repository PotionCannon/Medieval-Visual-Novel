DRAGONS, RIDERS, HEART CRYSTALS

Four dragon riders and their dragons formerly shared a great tower. A rider and dragon form a profound spiritual connection: not telepathy, but an almost wordless mutual knowing and deep devotion. Riders have little room for other intimate bonds while joined to their dragon. The Draconian has been bonded for several years; exact count not important.

CULTURAL CUSTOM: “Dragons live by fire, but you bury them in water.” At a water funeral, the dragon's body sinks and its departing spirit condenses into a hard glowing HEART CRYSTAL beneath the water. Most riders leave the crystal there to honor the fallen companion. The Draconian instead retrieves his dragon's blue crystal to prove the death and bring his companion into the confrontation. Later he entrusts it to his brothers for proper rebirth.

CRYSTAL COLOR AND REBIRTH (BACKGROUND LORE, NOT EXPLANATORY DIALOGUE): A heart crystal's color reflects the purity of the rider's heart and duration of the bond. A dragon long bound to a wicked rider can yield a RED crystal filled with hate. Over time it can gradually turn PURPLE, then BLUE, before a new life cycle as another spiritual dragon. The red dragon's crystal begins changing red-to-purple in the post-credits scene.

The dragons communicate among themselves. The two surviving dragons recognize the significance of the blue crystal and doubt the red dragon's account. Dragon perch architecture: upper communal chamber, four private chambers, each with a launch/landing perch, inaccessible from the human stairwell to the dragons themselves.
