CHARACTERS — NAMES NOT YET CHOSEN

THE HEALER — Female human (species may be revisited if desired), main playable character for town days. Experienced spiritual healer. Kind to townspeople and the stranger alike; guided by integrity, compassionate without being passive. Her care for the rider grows into true friendship and love. She strongly cautions against vengeance while understanding his loyalty to his two other comrades. Her medical facility is a ward/establishment; precise building type not fixed.

THE APPRENTICE — Young male, not related by blood to the healer; she treats him like a younger brother. NOT a love interest. Capable of healing cuts, bruises, and very minor deeper cuts, but not severe penetrating/internal injuries. Trusted with responsibility; day-three tavern escort is within his authority. Learns that self-knowledge and integrity matter before technique. Ultimately takes charge of the establishment when the healer prepares to depart.

THE DRACONIAN RIDER — Male scaled humanoid; “Draconian” is a working species term. Dragon rider and warrior, jovial at times but also reserved. Deeply bonded to his dragon for years. That bond leaves little room for other intimate relationships. Severely injured in a deliberate attack; grieves his dragon and seeks revenge. Gradually recognizes the healer as a true friend because she loves everyone around her, including him. POV character on day three and for the later journey and tower arc.

THE DECEASED DRAGON — Spiritually bonded companion of the Draconian; dies in the crash. Its heart crystal glows BLUE. The rider retrieves the crystal at the funeral and later entrusts it to his brothers.

THE RED RIDER — Human male, traitor, rider of the red dragon. Secretly arranges a two-rider training flight to attack the Draconian, then tells the others a false version of events. Mocks the survivor at the tower. Dies when his dragon is shot down during the confrontation.

THE GREEN RIDER — One of the two surviving comrades, dressed in green. Initially ready to draw against the Draconian, but restrained by the tan rider. Eventually embraces his brother after seeing the heart crystal.

THE TAN RIDER — Other surviving comrade, dressed in tan. Restrains green rider, asks questions, eventually accepts the truth and embraces the Draconian.

THE RED, GREEN AND TAN DRAGONS — Their riders' companions. The red dragon participates in the betrayal and attempts to deceive the others; the green and tan dragons turn against it after seeing the blue heart crystal. Red dragon's heart crystal appears red underwater and starts turning purple after credits.

THE BLACKSMITH — Town craftsman, with a left hand that is extraordinarily hot, able to increase its heat but not cool it below molten temperature. Repairs the Draconian's damaged sword, collected wrapped in cloth on day five.

THE TAVERNKEEPER — Runs an otherwise ordinary medieval tavern. Has a mechanical RIGHT hand/arm attachment, decorative and not a normal working hand; clockwork mechanisms powered by gears, springs and levers perform special functions, including a hidden wrist flask. Shares strong drink with the rider on day three.

OTHER TOWNSPEOPLE — Patients, market sellers, families and other residents; identities and roles to develop later.
