MEDIEVAL ROMANCE VISUAL NOVEL — STORY BIBLE (WORKING TITLE)
Planning archive, 14 November 2026.
Status: outline, not a finished screenplay. Unnamed characters and places remain unnamed intentionally.

CORE: A linear, character-driven medieval fantasy romance about genuine, self-giving love, friendship, loyalty, grief, and choosing another's good over one's own desires. Love is revealed through actions, not a relationship score or lust.

GAME: Traditional point-and-click visual novel: illustrated backgrounds, character portraits, standard dialogue box, clickable interactions, occasional choices and simple activities. First-person internal thoughts appear IN THE SAME STANDARD TEXT BOX as spoken dialogue, distinguished by a different font/style, not separate speech bubbles. Day three is PLAYED from the Draconian rider's perspective. The story's major events and ending are fixed.
