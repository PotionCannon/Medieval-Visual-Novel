STORY TIMELINE — ESTABLISHED EVENTS

DAY ONE — THE CRASH (HEALER POV)
The female healer begins her ordinary day, preparing her medical establishment and instructing her unrelated male apprentice. A severely wounded scaled Draconian dragon rider crashes near town into a stable/barn. His dragon has already died when she reaches it. The rider is briefly conscious; in pain he asks, “My dragon...” The healer clasps his hand and looks down, communicating the death without words. She saves his life. He then remains unconscious for the rest of day one. His weapon is taken into safekeeping. The day marker should say Day One.

DAY TWO — AWAKENING (HEALER POV)
The rider is conscious but bedridden. The healer carries out ordinary medical duties, teaches her apprentice integrity and intention before technique, and speaks at his bedside. He asks about his sword; she assures him it is safe. This is the first substantial conversation in which the healer learns about the rider. The apprentice can practice only basic healing. The rider's dragon was his deeply devoted, spiritually bonded companion, not merely a mount. The dragon rider knows the attack was deliberate, and he wants revenge. The healer cautions against vengeance but recognizes his loyalty and obligations toward the other two riders. Day Two marker.

DAY THREE — TAVERN (DRACONIAN POV — PLAYABLE)
The rider can get up and move, though still recovering. He asks to visit the town tavern. The healer is away tending townspeople, and the apprentice has been entrusted with his care; he judges that he can escort the rider. They meet the tavernkeeper, whose decorative clockwork right hand is not a normally functioning prosthetic. Gears, springs, and levers power special tools, including a hidden wrist bottle of strong drink. The tavernkeeper pours himself and the rider a glass; the rider coughs at its strength. Apprentice and rider bond. When the healer returns, she gently gives the apprentice a little grief for taking her patient drinking, without genuine anger. She trusts him and sees that the rider is fine. The healer notices the rider's sword is damaged and arranges for the blacksmith to repair it off-screen around this period. Day Three marker.

DAY FOUR — NIGHTMARE (DRACONIAN FLASHBACK, THEN BEDSIDE)
Open in the sky, riding beside a HUMAN rider on a RED dragon. Their flight begins as a training outing, privately arranged by the red rider rather than with all four riders. The red rider intentionally attacks, shoots the Draconian and his dragon from the sky, and departs believing them dead. The Draconian relives the impact and jolts awake from the nightmare. The apprentice at the bedside calls for the healer. She arrives; he explains it was only a nightmare. She prepares tea, offers him a cup, and he refuses. She drinks tea while asking him questions; the apprentice is present, interrupts, and asks his own questions. This differs from the day-two conversation. The scene ends with the healer smiling, the apprentice waving, and her saying, “Well, there's much to do today, so you get some rest.” They leave; fade to black, then Day Five.

DAY FIVE — THE HEALER'S ROUNDS (HEALER POV)
Follow the healer's ordinary daily tasks around town. At the market, she buys genuine real-world herbs with documented historical medicinal use; they should be researched as needed and woven naturally into dialogue, without clinical lectures. She visits the blacksmith and collects the repaired sword, wrapped in cloth, without revealing its identity directly to the player. She does not return it yet. Day Five marker.

DAY SIX — WATER FUNERAL AND SWORD (HEALER POV)
The deceased dragon is given a culturally traditional water burial: dragons live by fire but are buried in water. As its spirit departs the body, it becomes a hard glowing heart crystal that sinks beneath the water. Most riders leave the crystal undisturbed, but this rider retrieves his companion's crystal: to prove the dragon's death and carry the companion into his confrontation. After the funeral, the healer presents his repaired sword. He is grateful; grief and renewed resolve to confront his betrayer take hold. This begins harder conversations between them about vengeance and duty. They are developing true friendship and affection; she increasingly checks on him because she wants his company, not only because of medical necessity. Day Six marker.

DAY THIRTY — THE DEPARTURE (24 DAYS AFTER DAY SIX)
Display “30 Days After the Crash.” The rider has stayed in town to heal and has seen the healer's genuine care for everyone, including a stranger. The healer packs a medical satchel and entrusts her establishment to her apprentice, intending to accompany him. As she leaves, she meets the Draconian, who has come to say farewell. She tells him she intends to come to help if he is hurt. He looks past her toward the apprentice, places a hand gently on her shoulder and says: “You are needed here. I am needed elsewhere. There will be a time when we come back together and meet again. And I hope that it will be sooner rather than later.” She struggles to hold back tears, places her hand on his, looks sternly into his eyes and says, “Soon.” Gameplay POV switches from healer to Draconian for the journey and tower confrontation.

AFTER DAY THIRTY
See 02_ending.txt. The journey to the tower and return happen after the departure. The homecoming occurs three days after the red rider/dragon funeral, after a total absence of a little under two weeks.
